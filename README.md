## 📖 About

**Code in Place** is a free introductory programming course offered by Stanford University, based on its flagship course **CS106A – Programming Methodologies**. Originally launched in 2020 during the pandemic, it has since reached over **30,000 learners** around the world across multiple iterations.

The course is taught in **Python** and is designed for complete beginners — no prior coding experience required. It's open to anyone aged 16 and above, regardless of background.

🔗 **Official site:** [https://codeinplace.stanford.edu](https://codeinplace.stanford.edu)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🆓 **Completely free** — no cost, no hidden fees
- 🌍 **Fully online** — accessible from anywhere in the world
- 🧑‍🏫 **Human-centered** — small sections (~10 students) led by volunteer teachers
- 📅 **6 weeks** of structured learning with live lectures and assignments
- 🏆 **Stanford Certificate** upon successful completion
- 🤝 **Community-powered** — volunteer section leaders from diverse industries and backgrounds

---

## 🗓️ Course Structure

The course runs for **6 weeks** and covers the following units:

| Unit | Topic |
|------|-------|
| **Unit 1** | Karel the Robot — Introduction to programming logic |
| **Unit 2** | Console Programming — Variables, control flow, loops, conditionals |
| **Unit 3** | Graphics, Lists, and Dictionaries |
| **Week 6** | Diagnostic exam and/or Final Project |

---

## 🧠 What You'll Learn

- Control flow (conditionals and loops)
- Console programs with variables
- Functions and decomposition
- Graphics and interactive visuals
- Lists and dictionaries
- Problem-solving and computational thinking

---

## 📜 Certificate Requirements

To earn a **Stanford Certificate of Completion**, students must:

- ✅ Attend at least **4 out of 6** live section meetings
- ✅ Complete the **diagnostic exam** in Week 6, **or** submit a **final project**

### 🏅 Optional Badges

| Badge | Requirement |
|-------|------------|
| Section Participation | Attend at least 6 live sections |
| Diagnostic Badge | Complete the course exam |
| Final Project Badge | Submit a final project |
| TeachNow | Complete at least 3 sessions as a Peer Helper |

---

## 👩‍💻 Application Process

1. Complete a short **Karel the Robot** programming task
2. Write a brief **personal introduction** (who you are and why you want to join)
3. If accepted, you unlock: live classes, weekly assignments, a global peer community, and a dedicated section leader

## 🌐 Who Is It For?

Code in Place is designed for:
